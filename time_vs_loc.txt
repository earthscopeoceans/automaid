A pressure row describes one observation at a reported instrument time. A
waveform row describes a segment of samples on a UTC time grid, defined by its
corrected first-sample time, sample rate, and sample count.

The waveform row’s estimated position refers to that first-sample time. The
pressure row’s estimated position refers to the reported observation time. Both
can be evaluated along the same dive trajectory.

Only waveform timestamps receive a clock correction. The float’s position at the
corrected first-sample time is estimated along a trajectory constructed from GPS
fixes and thermocline crossings inferred from pressure measurements at their
measured times.
